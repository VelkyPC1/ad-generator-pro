# Argument-First Philosophy

Source: Mark Builds Brands

## Key Principle

In the internet marketing world, everything is about the "magnificence of words" — power words, hyperbolic phrases, "astonishing", "steroid-like", "tsunami of buyers."

In the Agora world, everything is about the "magnificence of the argument."

## The Difference

- **IM approach:** Compelling WORDS → power word lists, flowery phrases, hyperbole
- **Agora approach:** Compelling ARGUMENTS → rock-solid logical and emotional arguments

## What This Means in Practice

Stop writing copy. Start crafting arguments.

Don't sit down with a "copy hat" and look for more power words. Start by building rock-solid, airtight logical and emotional arguments. Prove to people that you have something different and better.

Words come later — through the editing process you'll add stronger verbs and better language. But words WITHOUT foundation are just a pile of power words.

## Unique Mechanism

Always present a unique mechanism in some form — a new, different, and better way to achieve the result.

- I don't want to talk about a commodity (a solution available anywhere)
- I want to talk about a proprietary solution (something they can only get from me)
- I don't want to be an educator who inspires people to buy somewhere else
- I want to present a proprietary solution and show what makes it different and better

## North Star = Belief

Every marketing campaign is about leading a prospect on a journey. Where am I leading them? To a belief they must have BEFORE I present the offer.

This belief is the North Star. Everything I do, say, show — leads the prospect step by step to this belief.

Without structure and without this North Star, you never know:
- Whether you're on track or off track
- Whether you're leading the prospect in the right direction
- Whether you've said enough or not

## Verdict

Be argument creators, not copywriters. Craft arguments first and foremost.
